Does buying a stock the moment Trump praises it on Truth Social actually work?

Every documented Truth Social stock-praise post, entered at a price you could really buy after the post, using real daily closes.

Bottom line: no. On the 7 clean, named-company praise posts, buying the post-day close returned an average of −1.77% the next day and −1.23% over a week — a 29% next-day win rate (only 2 of 7 went up). The strategy lost money. And the initial pop is gone in milliseconds anyway (see below), so retail never catches it.

Why you can't catch the pop: it happens in milliseconds

Milliseconds. Trump Media's new Truth API (launching Aug 1, 2026) delivers his posts to banks & trading firms "in milliseconds," built explicitly for algo firms "most impacted by the cost of a delay."
Seconds = the whole edge. Per the reporting, getting the post "even a few seconds faster" can mean "potentially massive profits" for algorithmic traders. The entire paid product only exists because speed is the trade.
You're last in line. A push notification + reading it + tapping buy is tens of seconds to minutes. By then the millisecond algos have already traded and the initial spike is over — so retail is too slow for the up-move and left holding into the fade. That's why the next-day numbers above are flat-to-negative.

Honesty & method

  • Sample is small & complete, not a sample of a bigger set. CNN's widely-cited "44 purchases / 21 companies" is an un-published AI-scan aggregate — CNN never printed the 21-company list, and only ever individually named ~12 (7 of them clean Truth-Social stock praise). So n=7 is the complete documented set, gathered by scraping the full CNN article + its embedded graphics + the Truth Social post archive.
  • Entry timing is honest. Each post's exact timestamp was pinned; post during market hours → same-day close; after 4pm ET / weekend → next trading day (the Aug 24 F-22 post was a Sunday → Aug 25 entry). Never a pre-post price.
  • Excluded: Dell (Trump praised it at a rally + White House event, not on Truth Social); the "GREAT TIME TO BUY!!!" post (named no company).
