Does buying a stock the moment Trump praises it on Truth Social actually work?

Every documented Truth Social stock-praise post, timestamped to the minute, entered at the first price you could really buy after the post — real daily closes.

Bottom line: it's a coin flip — and you can't catch it anyway. Buying at the timing-correct entry returned about +0.07% next-day on the 7 clean named-company posts (71% win) and +0.74% across the full 12-post set (75% win) — marginally positive, but carried by a couple winners and swinging to negative if you enter a day off. The real finding is that fragility: the answer moves several points on entry timing to the minute. And the initial pop clears in milliseconds (below), so a human never captures even that.

The headline numbers

Entry timing matters more than anything: my first pass with calendar-date entry got −1.77% next-day; using each post's real timestamp to place the entry correctly moved it to roughly flat. "Clean core" = 7 posts naming a specific public company; "generous set" folds in 4 weaker cases (an F-22 jet post that didn't name the makers, a Dell-family donation post, a 2026 Palantir post).

Why you can't catch the pop: it happens in milliseconds

Milliseconds. Trump Media's new Truth API (launching Aug 1, 2026) delivers his posts to banks & trading firms "in milliseconds," built explicitly for algo firms "most impacted by the cost of a delay."
Seconds = the whole edge. Per the reporting, getting the post "even a few seconds faster" can mean "potentially massive profits" for algorithmic traders. The entire paid product only exists because speed is the trade.
You're last in line. A push notification + reading it + tapping buy is tens of seconds to minutes. By then the millisecond algos have already traded and the initial spike is over — so even the marginal, fragile edge above is not one a human collects.

Honesty & method

  • Complete documented set, not a sample. CNN's widely-cited "44 purchases / 21 companies" is an un-published AI-scan aggregate — CNN never printed the 21-company list and only ever individually named ~12 (8 clean praise posts + an F-22 trio + 2 negative posts). n=12 priced is the complete documented set, gathered by scraping the full CNN article + its embedded graphics + the Truth Social post archive; every post is timestamped to the minute from trumpstruth.org.
  • The number is entry-timing-sensitive and set-sensitive — treat the ~flat clean-core figure as the honest signal and +1.69% as the generous one.
  • Excluded / flagged: Dell's famous "buy a Dell" lines were said at rallies + a White House event, not on Truth Social (its one real TS post praises the Dell family's donation, not the stock — priced as borderline); the "GREAT TIME TO BUY!!!" post named no company; Microsoft/Comcast posts were criticism, not praise.
